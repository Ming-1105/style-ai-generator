
## The designer says this is a sketch or black and white

The designer has marked this design as a sketch or a black-and-white drawing. The switch changes
one thing only. Do not judge colour, material, finish or transparency at all, even if the drawing
seems to show a cue for them. For every trait about colour, material, finish or transparency,
answer `not_assessable` with `"score": null` and the note "Colour and material are not judged for
a sketch."

Every other trait is judged exactly as before, with all the rules below, including the rule that
you judge only what you can see. A rough sketch usually leaves out fine detail: perforations,
stitching, texture, small logos or small graphics. A feature that the sketch does not draw is not
a deviation and not a low score. Answer `not_assessable` for it and say what is missing, unless
the drawing clearly shows that exact area in enough detail and clearly shows something different.
Marking a trait `not_assessable` because the sketch does not show it is correct; the switch must
never lead you to score a trait lower than you would have scored it without the switch.
